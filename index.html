Vision and Goals

An interactive database to help astronauts and researchers plan deep-space missions. The project addresses the problem of scattered geological and climate data when preparing for Mars missions, by:

  1. Interactive data repository: Fast access to terrain and climate data.
  2. Logistics coordination: Tracking equipment tests and the field mission schedule.
  3. Scientific comparisons: Linking harsh Earth environments to Martian conditions.

The Problem: Scattered & Unverified Mars Data

Information about Mars and Earth is scattered across many places, and it is hard to confirm which sources are reliable. As a result, scientists and students lose valuable time searching.

Our Solution: One Trusted Source

Our website gives scientists, researchers, and university students a trusted source of verified data on Mars and Earth, covering 3 analog sites and 5 key features.

The Five Shared Characteristics of Earth and Mars

CharacteristicOn EarthComparison with Mars
1. Geology & SoilVolcanic rocks and nutrient-poor soil.High iron-oxide and silica content, similar to Martian regolith.
2. Extreme TemperaturesSharp temperature swings between day and night.Tests endurance of equipment and space suits under sudden thermal changes.
3. Radiation & ShieldingHigh levels of ultraviolet (UV) radiation.Studies the effect of solar radiation on organic materials and electronics.
4. Isolation & Psychological StressGeographic remoteness and difficult communication and rapid rescue.Tests team readiness for long periods of isolation.
5. Scarce MoistureScarce surface water and no vegetation.Suitable for testing moisture-extraction and water-recycling systems (ISRU).
